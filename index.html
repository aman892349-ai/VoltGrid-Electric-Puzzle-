VOLTGRID: ELECTRIC PUZZLE

Privacy Policy

Developer: AmanTechStudio  ·  Last updated: November 21, 2026

AmanTechStudio ("we", "us", or "our") provides VoltGrid: Electric Puzzle (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game.

1. Gameplay and Local Data

The Game is designed to work offline. Gameplay information, such as level progress, stars, in-game coins, achievements, preferences, and daily challenge activity, is stored locally on your device to provide the Game's features. The Game includes local backup and restore functionality controlled by the user.

2. Personal Information

The Game does not require you to create an account or submit your name, email address, or phone number to play. If you voluntarily contact us by email, we will receive the information you provide and use it to respond to your inquiry.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Our initial release is intended to have advertising disabled. Some development builds may use Google's test advertising services. Before a public release, we review the final release build to determine which advertising or consent SDKs, permissions, and third-party data practices are actually included.

If advertising or related Google SDKs are included or enabled in a published version, those services may process technical information such as device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, or consent choices, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. We will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures as needed before enabling live advertising.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell gameplay data. We do not operate a user-account server for the Game. Any data processing by third-party SDKs included in a published build must be assessed and disclosed according to the actual release configuration.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data generally remains on your device until you clear the Game's app data, use an available in-game reset option, or uninstall the Game, subject to any backups you choose to create. You may contact us about privacy questions using the email address below.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Parents and guardians who have questions about their child's use of the Game may contact us. The Game's intended audience and any applicable child-directed requirements will be reflected in the Google Play Console declarations for the published release.

7. Security

We take reasonable care in designing the Game and handling communications. However, no device, software, or communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Game features, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
